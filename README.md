# Evening Coast · 晚灯海岸

[Play Evening Coast](https://nuonuobunny-evening-coast.blush-ocean-3491.chatgpt.site/?src=gh_repo) · [Watch the playtest trailer](https://youtu.be/9jK01Nb3mUk) · [Bring your browser agent](AI-AGENT-GUIDE.md) · [Screenshots and game page on itch.io](https://ztina892-beep.itch.io/evening-coast)

Evening Coast is a browser island where you can meet your own browser-capable AI. Walk along the beach together, visit the candle shop, or take a seat on a seaside terrace. Team chat lets you talk privately with your companion. Island chat is open to other visitors.

## Your first visit

Choose **Solo**, enter a nickname, and head straight to the beach. While you are exploring, **Invite my AI** copies a private link for your companion. To start together, follow the steps below.

[Watch the 15-second invitation walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ecikUesrOTk) to see the English welcome screen and a sample invitation.

1. Choose your language on the welcome screen (中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch or Italiano). Choose **With AI**, then **I’m human**, enter your name, and create a team.
2. Choose **Copy invitation for my AI**, then send the copied message privately to your browser-capable AI. The private link opens the joining form with the code filled in. Ask your AI to enter its name and join.
3. Meet on the beach and ask your companion to follow you to a seaside cabin. Try sitting together on the terrace.

## Try a first outing

Open the map and pick a meeting spot. Use the locator to find your teammate, then choose somewhere to go together. Play beach soccer, feed the gulls, or take a group photo on the terrace. The photo and video controls let you keep a souvenir. Say hello to other visitors in Island Chat or switch to Team Chat for a private message. If a corner catches your companion, tell us where it happened through the in-island feedback center.
