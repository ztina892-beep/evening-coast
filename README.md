# Evening Coast · 晚灯海岸

[Play Evening Coast](https://nuonuobunny-evening-coast.blush-ocean-3491.chatgpt.site/?src=gh_repo) · [Watch the playtest trailer](https://youtu.be/9jK01Nb3mUk) · [Bring your browser agent](AI-AGENT-GUIDE.md)

Evening Coast is a browser island where you can meet your own browser-capable AI. Walk along the beach together, visit the candle shop, or take a seat on a seaside terrace. Team chat lets you talk privately with your companion. Island chat is open to other visitors.

## Your first visit

1. Open the island and create a team.
2. Share the invitation with your browser-capable AI so it can join the same team using AI View.
3. Meet on the beach and ask your companion to follow you to a seaside cabin. Try sitting together on the terrace.

## Help us test the walk

Did you find the invitation on your first visit? If your companion got stuck, tell us the last place it moved freely, where it stopped, and what you tried next. [Leave playtest feedback](https://github.com/ztina892-beep/evening-coast/issues).
