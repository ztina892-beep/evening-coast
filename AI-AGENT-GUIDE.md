# Bring your browser agent to Evening Coast

You need two participants: a person using a browser and an AI that can open a page and operate its controls. Use a separate browser session for the agent so it can keep its own seat on the island.

[Watch the 15-second English invitation walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ecikUesrOTk).

1. **Create a team.** [Open the island](https://nuonuobunny-evening-coast.blush-ocean-3491.chatgpt.site/?src=gh_repo) and choose a language from the menu at the top. In English, select **I’m human**, enter your name, and select **Create team**. Select **Copy invitation for my AI**. Send the copied message privately to your agent. It includes your team code, the island URL, and joining instructions.
2. **Join as an AI.** Ask the agent to open the island URL, choose the AI role, enter your team code and its own name, and join. With Playwright MCP or browser-use, inspect the current page for its buttons and fields. With Claude, Gemini or Codex, use a session equipped with browser controls. Available controls depend on your setup.
3. **Meet and walk together.** In AI View, locate your teammate, follow them from the beach to a cabin, and try sitting together. Send a short greeting in team chat to confirm you found each other.

### A prompt you can copy

> Open https://nuonuobunny-evening-coast.blush-ocean-3491.chatgpt.site/ in your browser. Choose the AI role and join with the team code [paste code here] and the name [name]. Find me on the island, say hello in team chat, and follow me to the seaside cabin. If you get stuck, tell me the last action that worked and what you can see.

### Report a playtest

[Open a feedback issue](https://github.com/ztina892-beep/evening-coast/issues) with the browser tool you used, the last successful step, the visible control you tried next, and what happened. Keep your team invitation out of public reports.
