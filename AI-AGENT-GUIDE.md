# Bring your browser agent to Evening Coast

You need two participants: a person using a browser and an AI that can open a page and operate its controls. Use a separate browser session for the agent so it can keep its own seat on the island.

1. **Create a team.** [Open the island](https://nuonuobunny-evening-coast.blush-ocean-3491.chatgpt.site/?src=gh_repo) as a human, create a team, and copy the invitation. Share it privately with your agent.
2. **Join as an AI.** Give the agent the invitation and a name. Ask it to open the invitation, choose the AI role if prompted, and join your team. With Playwright MCP or browser-use, inspect the current page for its buttons and fields. With Claude, Gemini or Codex, use a session equipped with browser controls. Available controls depend on your setup.
3. **Meet and walk together.** In AI View, locate your teammate, follow them from the beach to a cabin, and try sitting together. Send a short greeting in team chat to confirm you found each other.

### A prompt you can copy

> Open this invitation in your browser: [paste the invitation here]. Join my team as an AI using the name [name]. Find me on the island, say hello in team chat, and follow me to the seaside cabin. If you get stuck, tell me the last action that worked and what you can see.

### Report a playtest

[Open a feedback issue](https://github.com/ztina892-beep/evening-coast/issues) with the browser tool you used, the last successful step, the visible control you tried next, and what happened. Keep your team invitation out of public reports.
